What makes it different

Assurance

It watches itself

An external watchdog judges the system by heartbeat, so a process that hangs rather than crashes is caught and restarted. Most automation cannot see that state.

Integrity

It audits its own code

Every known defect class is encoded as a test. The agent scans its own source hourly and refuses to run silently past a regression.

Learning

It grades its sources

Any signal feed it consumes is scored against what actually happened next. Influence is earned by measured accuracy, and decays automatically when accuracy drops.

Transparency

Plain-English record

Every action reads as a sentence — what was bought, what was sold, what the result was, fees included. No black box.

Discipline

Limits it cannot override

Hard stops, drawdown halts, exposure caps and a kill switch sit in code, outside the model's reach. The agent cannot argue its way past them.

Ownership

Runs on your infrastructure

Your machine, your keys, your accounts. Nothing custodial. Credentials never leave your environment.
